How a task moves

01

Assigned

The manager splits the brief into tasks and hands each one to a team.

02

Branched

A branch opens with the task. Everything after this happens in the repo.

03

Pushed

Commits land against the task. Every learner's share is counted as it goes.

04

Reviewed

Two teammates read it first. You only see what survives that.

05

Closed

Merged, marked, and written back to the learner's record.
